How RoadGuard AI Works

A complete citizen-to-repair pipeline

1. Capture

Upload or click a photo of the damaged road.

2. AI Analysis

Computer vision detects potholes, severity and estimated area.

3. Locate & Report

GPS is captured, a complaint is auto-registered and duplicates are merged.

4. Repair

Government prioritizes, assigns a team, and AI verifies the repair.

About the Project

RoadGuard AI is a full-stack road-infrastructure management platform:

  • Computer vision pothole detection with severity & area estimation
  • Automatic complaint registration with GPS + duplicate detection
  • Deterministic repair-cost estimation from configurable rates
  • Government dashboard with analytics, priority & road health
  • Repair-team workflow with before/after AI verification
  • GenAI repair reports and a government analytics assistant

Built with FastAPI, PostgreSQL/PostGIS, vanilla JS, Bootstrap 5, Chart.js and Leaflet.

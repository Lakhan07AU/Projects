Dear Sister

💌 Dear Sister,

Today, on your birthday, I sat down to write you a letter and realised I don't have enough words. No poem is long enough. No music is beautiful enough. No gift is precious enough for someone like you.

I have watched you — the little girl who chased butterflies in our garden, the student who burned midnight oil and still woke up smiling, the daughter who held Mama's hand in tough times, the sister who always made sure everyone else was okay before herself.

I have seen you cry in silence when you didn't want to worry anyone. I have seen you laugh so loudly that the whole room lit up. I have seen you carry the weight of the world on your shoulders and still walk gracefully.

You are not just my sister. You are my first friend, my safe place, my inspiration, my home.

Thank you for every fight we had — because every fight was proof that we care. Thank you for every hug — because every hug felt like the safest place on earth. Thank you for every smile — because it made my world beautiful. Thank you for every sacrifice you made quietly, for this family, for me.

"तू मेरी जान है। तू मेरा गर्व है। तू मेरी दुनिया है।"
"You are my life. You are my pride. You are my world."

May this year bring you everything your heart secretly wishes for. May you laugh more than you cry. May you achieve everything you dream of. May every morning feel like a gift.

With all my love, always yours — Your Brother / Sister ❤️
